01 — What we do

The whole process.
Under one roof.

Everything from buying the raw stock to the packaged product is done in our own shop. No chasing one supplier for cutting, another for machining and another for coating — we handle the whole job, and you deal with a single deadline.

01

Material cutting

A metal stock warehouse served by a gantry crane, CNC band saws and manual circular saws for precise cutting of profiles and sheets.

Cut · Saw
02

Forming & pressing

Manual and NC press brakes, profile rolling machines and a wide range of eccentric presses with NC feeders.

Bend · Roll · Press
03

CNC machining

A toolroom of lathes, mills and grinders, plus CNC machining centres including a production horizontal pallet mill and an 8-axis centre.

Turn · Mill · Grind
04

Welding

Fully equipped welding stations including a CNC pallet welding robot for repeatable, precise welds.

MIG · TIG · Robotic
05

Surface finishing

High-capacity blasting as pre-treatment, an automatic powder-coating line, plus grinding and polishing.

Blast · Powder coat · Polish
06

3D printing & prototyping

Prototype 3D printing with support for advanced technical materials — ABS/ASA, nylon, polycarbonate and TPU.

ABS/ASA · Nylon · PC · TPU
02 — The facility

Since 1994 — from raw stock to finished part.

We run a metal stock warehouse with a gantry crane linked to cutting stations, a toolroom, CNC machining centres, welding bays including a robotic cell, and an automatic powder-coating line. We hold our own storage for packaging and finished goods with a loading ramp — the entire chain stays in-house.
