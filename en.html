Insurance claims

Leave the handling of insurance claims and all the necessary formalities to us. We will arrange the full repair of a vehicle insured with any insurance company – you just bring us the car and drive away with it repaired.

We are a contractual partner of insurance companies. For the duration of the repair we offer you a replacement vehicle rental, so you will not be left without a car.
